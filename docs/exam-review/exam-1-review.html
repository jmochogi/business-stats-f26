Question 1 - Data & Study Design

A prominent investment firm based in the US wants to evaluate the portfolio management efficiency of its financial analysts. To conduct the study, 150 financial analysts were randomly selected from the firm’s workforce. Among those sampled, 65 were classified as junior analysts, having less than five years of experience, while the remaining 85 were classified as senior analysts, with five or more years of experience. The analysts were responsible for managing investments across three key market sectors: technology, energy, and financial services.

The firm’s primary focus was on how quickly analysts were able to generate a profitable return on a trade. On average, junior analysts required 3 days to close a profitable trade, whereas senior analysts were able to do so in about 2 days on average. Beyond trade execution speed, the firm also gathered additional information on each analyst, including their gender (male/female), their highesl level of education, and the average total value (in millions of dollars) of the portfolios under their management.

  1. Classify this scenario as an observational or experimental study. Give a reason for your classification.

  2. Identify the sample and the population of interest.

  3. What are the cases/observational units in this study and how many are they?

  4. Identify any one parameter and its associated statistic in this study, stating the specific values for each if given.

  5. List all the variables in the study and identify each variable as numerical or categorical. For each numerical variable state whether it is discrete or continuous. For each categorical variable state whether it is nominal or ordinal.

  6. Identify a variable (or variable set if more than one) that can be reasonably visualized using:

    • A simple bar plot
    • A simple histogram
    • Simple box plot
    • Side-by-side box plots
    • Side-by-side bar plots
    • Scatter plot

A business analyst wants to perform statistical inference to answer the research question,

Do arrival delay rates (proportions) differ between Delta and American Airlines?.

The Airlines data set from the Stat2Data package contains information on over 10,000 flights by Delta and American Airlines arriving at LaGuardia and O’Hare airports. These data are summarized as shown in the table below:

Airline Delayed Arrivals On-Time Arrivals Total
Delta 659 2344 3003
American 1450 5880 7330
Total 2109 8224 10333

  1. Identify the predictor and response variable in the above scenario.

  2. State the null and alternative hypotheses in words and in symbols.

  3. Calculate the observed statistic (i.e., the difference in delay proportions in the sample). Does the observed statistic suggest a difference in delay rates between the two airlines?
